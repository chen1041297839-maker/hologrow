FAQ

Agency client reporting, PPC reviews and report delivery FAQ

How do we connect client accounts?

Use authorization from an account permitted to access the relevant resources. Start with one client and verify the connection, scope, and tables before analysis.

Can we retain client-specific reporting definitions?

Supply the client’s goals, budget, period, and metric definitions to the AI analysis. Reusable Skills provide a process; they do not make different client definitions interchangeable.

Does Hologrow include a white-label reporting portal?

This workflow prepares reports in your connected AI tool. It does not promise a white-label portal or automatic delivery to clients; your team reviews and shares outputs.

What happens when authorization expires?

Reauthorize the affected connection and verify the latest successful sync before using it in a report. Mark unavailable data rather than treating it as zero.

Can I generate ecommerce client WBRs and QBRs?

Use the client’s authorized data, KPI definitions and agreed targets. The agent can draft changes, evidence and discussion questions; your team reviews recommendations before sharing.

How should a PPC agency handle attribution differences?

Keep each ad platform’s conversion window and revenue definition visible. Compare spend and commerce outcomes without summing potentially overlapping attributed sales.

How do we follow up on last week’s client action items?

Supply the previous action list, owners and agreed success measures. Ask the agent to compare new evidence with those measures and separate completed actions from unverified progress. The report does not automatically update your project-management system.

Can client reports be sent automatically?

Sending requires your agent’s supported delivery tools and authorization. Validate source coverage and the report first; the Hologrow data connection alone does not create a delivery schedule.

Can I use the same reporting prompt for different clients?

Reuse the analysis steps, while explicitly setting each client’s account scope, timezone, KPI glossary and targets. Do not mix client records or assume identical business goals.

How do I estimate monthly client budget pacing?

Combine actual spend with the client-supplied budget and calendar. Label constant-spend projections as scenarios, and account for planned promotions before recommending changes.
