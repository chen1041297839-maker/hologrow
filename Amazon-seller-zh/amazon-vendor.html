The 1P demand and supply disconnect

A retail decline can start in demand, availability or supply.

What makes the decision difficult

  1. 01The Vendor manager exports retail sales and traffic; the supply team checks inventory and forecasts. Different report dates obscure whether an ASIN lost demand or availability.
  2. 02A purchase order, a shipment and Amazon’s consumer sales represent different stages. Combining their quantities in one total creates a misleading view of the 1P business.
  3. 03A forecast changes between reviews. Without its issue date and target window, the team cannot tell a planning miss from a revised forecast.

What you can prepare with Hologrow

  • An ASIN-level view of retail changes alongside traffic and inventory.
  • Clearly separated retail and supply measures for a joint review.
  • A sourced brief that distinguishes observed signals from possible explanations.

FAQ

Vendor Central analytics and 1P reporting questions

How is Vendor analytics different from Seller analytics?

Vendor is a 1P relationship supplying Amazon; Seller is a 3P selling model. Retail sell-through and wholesale revenue must retain their own definitions and should not be added together.

Which Amazon Vendor Central reports can I use?

The connector covers sales, traffic, forecasts, and inventory reports. Other operational tables depend on the permissions and coverage visible in your workspace.

Why is a report missing?

Check the authorized vendor account, region, report permissions, available dates, and sync status. An authorized connection does not mean every report has finished syncing.

Can Hologrow manage purchase orders or recover chargebacks?

No. This workflow reads synchronized reports and prepares analysis for review. It does not submit claims or change purchase orders.

Does Vendor analytics combine wholesale and retail revenue?

No. Keep your shipments to Amazon separate from Amazon’s retail sales to consumers. They describe different transactions and reporting periods.

How should I compare Vendor glance views with retail sales?

Align ASIN, marketplace and complete periods, then compare traffic and unit changes alongside stock. The relationship suggests investigation directions; it does not prove a cause.

Can the agent prepare a Vendor Central weekly business review?

Yes, using available synchronized reports and a defined scope. A WBR can separate retail drivers, traffic, stock coverage, forecast changes and available supply exceptions.

Does a PO or receipt difference mean a chargeback is recoverable?

No. Differences are reconciliation leads. Validate line IDs, dates and receiving evidence before investigating further; automated claims or recovery outcomes are not promised.

Can I compare forecast revisions with actual sell-through?

When matching reports exist, retain the forecast vintage and compare the corresponding retail period. Missing history and zero actuals must be handled explicitly.

How do Vendor inventory coverage alerts work?

Use dated sellable-stock snapshots and recent retail velocity. Confirm thresholds and supply assumptions with your team; an open PO is not sellable inventory.
