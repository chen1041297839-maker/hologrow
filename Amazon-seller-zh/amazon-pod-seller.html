What makes the decision difficult

  1. 01A holiday illustration appears on tees and mugs, each with its own ASIN and variations. Rebuilding the design-to-SKU spreadsheet becomes part of every collection review.
  2. 02Ad reports organize spend by advertised products, while print and blank-product costs live in supplier files. Sales alone cannot tell you which collection earns a contribution margin.
  3. 03A new design’s first three orders compete with an established collection’s full month. Unequal launch ages and small samples can send the next creative budget in the wrong direction.

What you can prepare with Hologrow

  • A design-level view using your own SKU mapping.
  • Sales and advertising compared together, with cost gaps visible.
  • A test shortlist that retains sample size and observation period.

FAQ

Seller Central POD analytics and design reporting FAQ

Is this for Amazon Merch on Demand?

This page is for print on demand sellers using Seller Central. It does not promise a Merch on Demand, Printful, or Printify integration.

Where do design groups come from?

You supply the relationship between designs and SKUs to the analysis workflow. Amazon product records do not automatically provide your creative taxonomy.

Can I analyze products without production costs?

You can compare sales, units, advertising, and available refunds. Profit conclusions require sufficient production, fulfilment, fee, and refund inputs.

How are small samples handled?

Ask the AI to show order counts, dates, and missing history. Early results should inform a further test, not be presented as guaranteed winners.

Is this Amazon Merch on Demand analytics?

This page is for print-on-demand sellers operating through Seller Central. It does not promise access to Merch on Demand royalty reports or accounts.

Can I compare a design across shirts, mugs and variations?

Yes, when you supply a reliable design-to-SKU map. Keep product type and variation detail visible so different costs and sample sizes are not hidden by the aggregate.

Does Hologrow connect directly to Printful or Printify?

No such connection is claimed here. Production costs, supplier status and design metadata must come from context you supply through an appropriate supported workflow.

How do I compare Amazon PPC performance by design?

Join advertised ASINs with your design map. Retain ad attribution windows and show total order revenue separately. Unmapped products should remain visible instead of being silently excluded.

Can I create a seasonal POD portfolio report?

Supply the theme labels and event calendar, then compare matching pre-event and event windows. Limited history should be visible alongside test recommendations.

Will the agent generate or publish new designs?

These workflows analyze business performance. They do not promise design generation, intellectual-property clearance, catalog publishing or production orders.
