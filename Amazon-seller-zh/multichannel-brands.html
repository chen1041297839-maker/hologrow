When channel dashboards disagree

The leadership review starts by reconciling competing totals.

What makes the decision difficult

  1. 01Amazon and Shoplazza report orders; Google and Meta report attributed conversions. Adding every revenue column can count a purchase more than once.
  2. 02A product has an ASIN on Amazon and different shop SKUs elsewhere. Spreadsheets need mappings, refund rules and currency policies before the brand can compare its assortment.
  3. 03Marketing sees more GA4 sessions while operations sees flat orders. Without tracking coverage and matching periods, the team cannot decide whether to investigate acquisition or conversion.

FAQ

Multichannel ecommerce reporting and attribution questions

Which platforms can I connect today?

Documented integrations include Amazon Seller and Vendor, Shoplazza, Amazon Ads, Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4, Search Console, and Lingxing ERP. Coverage varies by account. Shopify, TikTok Shop, TEMU, Walmart, and Mercado Libre are listed as coming soon.

Can I add together each platform’s attributed revenue?

No. Attribution windows can overlap, and platforms may claim the same order. Keep order revenue and source-specific attributed revenue separate; this workflow does not promise causal attribution.

Can I calculate cross-channel profit?

Only with a clear definition and sufficient mapped costs, fees, refunds, and fulfilment inputs. Otherwise compare sales and spend while listing missing inputs.

What happens when a product is not mapped?

Keep it as an unmatched source-specific item. Provide or verify the mapping before combining product totals across channels.

Can I add Amazon, Meta and Google attributed revenue together?

No. Multiple platforms may claim the same conversion. Keep attributed revenue separate by source and use deduplicated, consistently defined order revenue for commerce totals.

Can I compare Amazon and Shoplazza product sales?

Use connected accounts and a supplied product mapping. Align dates, currencies and refund treatment before comparing ASINs with storefront SKUs.

How should I handle multi-currency ecommerce reporting?

Retain source currencies unless an exchange-rate source and conversion policy are supplied. Show the original and converted basis; do not silently mix currency values.

Does GA4 conversion reporting equal store orders?

No. Tracking coverage, consent, event definitions, timezones and attribution can differ. Compare analytics events and transactional orders as distinct measures.

Does this replace inventory transfers or order management?

No. These are read-only analysis workflows. Keep the operational systems that execute inventory transfers, fulfillment, returns and orders.

Can the agent prepare a monthly brand operating review?

Yes, from available connected sources, consistent definitions and your product map. Include channel revenue, ad spend, product movements and any missing-source caveats in the same brief.
