BEFORE YOU CONNECT

A few useful answers.

Your sources. Your control.

Review coverage and permissions before you connect. Hologrow reads the source data; your team stays in charge of changes.

Read the product documentation
Are Seller, Vendor and Ads the same connection?+

They are distinct sources with different data and permissions. Connect the sources you need in Integrations. Available tables depend on the authorized accounts, marketplaces, reports and source permissions.

Can I combine 1P, 3P and advertising data?+

Yes, where the relevant connections and business keys are available. Vendor wholesale revenue, Amazon retail sell-through, Seller sales and ad-attributed sales are different measures. Keep them separate and validate ASIN, marketplace, period and attribution mappings before comparison.

Does Amazon data alone establish profit?+

Not always. A contribution-profit analysis may also require effective product costs, shipping, duties and other business inputs. Use available cost data and validated mappings, and explicitly report missing costs instead of treating them as zero.

Can Hologrow change my accounts?+

No. Hologrow reads synchronized data. It does not change bids, budgets, listings, inventory or source-account settings. Analysis and recommendations remain for your team to review.

How fresh is the data?+

Hologrow runs scheduled incremental syncs and historical backfills. Report readiness depends on the source, account and table. Check the last successful sync and available date range in Data Explorer before comparing periods.
