BEFORE YOU CONNECT

A few useful answers.

Your sources. Your control.

Review coverage and permissions before you connect. Hologrow reads the source data; your team stays in charge of changes.

Read the product documentation
What do I need before connecting?+

You need a Hologrow account, permission to authorize the relevant Amazon account, at least one source syncing in Data Explorer, and a Claude workspace that permits connectors. Team or Enterprise workspaces may require an owner to add Hologrow.

Will Seller Central also connect my advertising data?+

Connect Amazon Ads separately when your question needs ad spend, campaign or keyword performance. Connecting Seller Central does not imply that advertising tables are available. Verify both sources in Data Explorer and Data Schema.

What if Claude returns no data?+

First check account authorization and sync status in Hologrow. Confirm that the requested table and date range are available, then verify the Claude connection. The first historical sync may still be running; missing or stale data should be reported before drawing a conclusion.

Can Hologrow change my accounts?+

No. Hologrow reads synchronized data. It does not change bids, budgets, listings, inventory or source-account settings. Analysis and recommendations remain for your team to review.

How fresh is the data?+

Hologrow runs scheduled incremental syncs and historical backfills. Report readiness depends on the source, account and table. Check the last successful sync and available date range in Data Explorer before comparing periods.
