06 / FAQ

Google Analytics 4 MCP integration FAQ

Is this a GA4 raw event warehouse?

No. The documented tables include daily overview, traffic, page and event metrics. Check Data Schema for dimensions and fields available to your property.

Can AI explain why GA4 and store revenue differ?

It can compare available evidence and identify checks: reporting dates, currency, purchase events and refunds. It cannot reconstruct uncollected events or prove causality from a correlation.

How do I connect Google Analytics 4 to Claude, ChatGPT or Codex?

Authorize Google Analytics 4 in Hologrow, confirm available tables and dates in Data Explorer, then follow your AI client's Hologrow setup guide. The AI queries synchronized data through Hologrow's hosted MCP connection.

How current is the Google Analytics 4 data?

Hologrow uses scheduled synchronization. Check the latest successful sync and actual date coverage for each table; first syncs and history can take time. Refresh frequency and available history depend on the source and report.
