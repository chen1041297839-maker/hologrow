01 / FROM A QUESTION TO EVIDENCE

What can AI investigate with Amazon Seller?

Three focused workflows. Each ends with something your team can check before acting.

01

Explain growth at the ASIN level

Use sales_and_traffic_child_asin_daily alongside order_items to locate the products driving the change. Keep ordered sales, shipped orders and traffic-based conversion definitions separate.

A product contribution analysis.
Ask your AI +

Which ASINs explain the sales change? Separate traffic from unit-session conversion.

02

Inspect the cost of a return spike

Review FBA return and shipment rows with available inventory snapshots. A return count is a signal to inspect a product or fulfillment issue, not proof of its cause.

A SKU-level operations review.
Ask your AI +

Which growing SKUs also show returns or low FBA stock in the available reports?

03

Bridge orders and money movement

Follow finance_transactions and settlement_report_rows to explain timing, fees and refunds. Add available advertising and cost data before attempting a margin calculation.

A reconciliation with missing cost items visible.
Ask your AI +

Explain the difference between order sales and settlements; do not call settlements profit.

WHEN REPORTS STAY DISCONNECTED

05 / SECURITY & CONTROL

Analyze Amazon Seller with read-only access.

A data connection supports investigation. It does not authorize operational changes through Hologrow.

Encrypted connection credentials

Authorize the intended Amazon Seller account. Hologrow stores connection credentials encrypted; verify the resulting account scope.

Read-only source tools

Hologrow provides read-only analysis of synchronized data. Listing edits, inventory actions and operational decisions remain outside these Hologrow tools.

06 / FAQ

Amazon Seller MCP integration FAQ

Does Seller Central authorization include Amazon Ads?

No. Connect Amazon Ads separately when you need campaign, keyword or Sponsored advertising reports. Match the marketplace and account scope before comparing results.

Can AI change listings or FBA inventory?

Hologrow provides read-only analysis of synchronized data. Listing edits, inventory actions and operational decisions remain outside these Hologrow tools.

How do I connect Amazon Seller to Claude, ChatGPT or Codex?

Authorize Amazon Seller in Hologrow, confirm available tables and dates in Data Explorer, then follow your AI client's Hologrow setup guide. The AI queries synchronized data through Hologrow's hosted MCP connection.

How current is the Amazon Seller data?

Hologrow uses scheduled synchronization. Check the latest successful sync and actual date coverage for each table; first syncs and history can take time. Refresh frequency and available history depend on the source and report.
