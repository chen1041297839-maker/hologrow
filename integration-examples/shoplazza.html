01 / FROM A QUESTION TO EVIDENCE

What can AI investigate with Shoplazza?

Three focused workflows. Each ends with something your team can check before acting.

01

Trace orders back to products

Review orders and order_line_items with products and product_variants. Distinguish product mix from changes in order count before claiming a stronger average basket.

A product-mix review.
Ask your AI +

Which products drove the order change, and what do available refund records show?

02

Inspect refunds beside payments

Query order_transactions and refund_records with their available identifiers. Keep order status, payment movement and refund timing explicit in the answer.

A payment and refund reconciliation.
Ask your AI +

03

Review acquisition against store outcomes

Add Google Ads or available Meta reporting and compare consistent periods. Use GA4 as behavioral context; campaign attribution requires a supported mapping, not just similar dates.

A spend-to-orders review with attribution limits.

03 / WHY HOLOGROW

Prepare Shoplazza for the questions a single report cannot answer.

Hologrow maintains the data connection and a queryable layer. Your AI can inspect records and definitions rather than relying on a pasted total.

WHEN REPORTS STAY DISCONNECTED

  1. 01Order growth can mask refunds and heavier discounting.
  2. 02Product, payment and analytics exports do not share the same row grain.
  3. 03Ads and storefront reports can disagree without either being broken.

PREPARED THROUGH HOLOGROW

  • Prepare transaction and refund evidence alongside the orders being reviewed.
  • Expose table fields and product / order keys so joins can be inspected.
  • Combine connected advertising and store data while documenting attribution and date assumptions.

05 / SECURITY & CONTROL

Analyze Shoplazza with read-only access.

A data connection supports investigation. It does not authorize operational changes through Hologrow.

Encrypted connection credentials

Authorize the intended Shoplazza account. Hologrow stores connection credentials encrypted; verify the resulting account scope.

Read-only source tools

Not through Hologrow’s read-only data tools. It can help identify records for review; operational changes remain in your store workflow.

Revoke AI access

Manage client authorization in AI Platforms. Disconnecting a source is separate: it stops sync and deletes its synchronized data.

Your AI client has its own data policies. Hologrow’s read-only boundary applies to the tools it provides.

06 / FAQ

Shoplazza MCP integration FAQ

Is this the same as a Shopify connector?

No. Shoplazza has its own connection and schema. This page describes Shoplazza orders, products, transactions, refunds and analytics rather than Shopify API coverage.

Can AI modify orders or issue refunds?

Not through Hologrow’s read-only data tools. It can help identify records for review; operational changes remain in your store workflow.

How do I connect Shoplazza to Claude, ChatGPT or Codex?

Authorize Shoplazza in Hologrow, confirm available tables and dates in Data Explorer, then follow your AI client's Hologrow setup guide. The AI queries synchronized data through Hologrow's hosted MCP connection.

How current is the Shoplazza data?

Hologrow uses scheduled synchronization. Check the latest successful sync and actual date coverage for each table; first syncs and history can take time. Refresh frequency and available history depend on the source and report.
